Documentation

Everything Barnark does, task by task.

Barnark keeps a new project’s architecture well shaped. It keeps a catalogue of architecture templates. You clone one into a template of your own, Barnark makes the project’s folder to it, and it checks at any time that the folders still keep to it. It is for folders only: it never looks at a file, and it is not tied to one style or one language.

The idea in five words #

  • Template. A description of what folders a project has and what each is for. Barnark ships five, read-only.
  • Clone. Copy a template under a name you give it and make its folder. Your copy is tied to nothing.
  • Ark. A named folder and the template that follows it, the bound template. A project can have many arks, anywhere on your computer. Other templates you keep are unbound.
  • Member. A folder you name inside a collection, such as a new Context. Adding one changes the template and the folders together.
  • Check. Compare the folder with its template. It reports what is missing and what is unexpected.
