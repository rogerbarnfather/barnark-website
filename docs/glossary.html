Glossary

One word, one meaning.

Every term Barnark uses, in alphabetical order. A word means one thing across this whole site, and matches the words in the browser app and on the command line.

Barn
A project with a .barn/ folder at its root, where Barnark keeps its records.
Ark
A named folder and the template that follows it. A project can have many. Its name is unique in the project, and its folder can be anywhere on your computer. The folder holds a small .barn-ark.json that points back to the project.
Bound template
The template that follows an ark’s folder. It is made together with the folder by a clone, and every change to it is a revision.
Check
Comparing an ark’s folder with its template, and reporting what is missing and what is unexpected. Files are never checked.
Clone
To copy a template under a name you give it, as a new ark’s template, and make the ark’s folder. The copy is tied to nothing.
Collection
A folder of a template that holds only members, all of one member type, such as Context in Domain Driven Design.
Component
A building block of a template: a folder with a meaning and its tags, holding fixed folders, a collection or nothing. It is placed when the template’s tree uses it, and only available when nothing does yet. template:components lists them, and the browser app’s Components view shows them. A component can be copied into one of your templates, where it is available until placed.
Copy
To make a new, unbound template of your own from a shipped template or one of yours, with no folder. Nothing is copied out of an ark. Changing one never changes the other.
Dry run
--dry-run on a clone: checks the template, the name and the target, and prints the folders it would make without writing anything.
Fixed folder
A folder the template names itself, such as Kernel or Api. Every folder made to the template has it.
Leaf
A folder of a template with no folders of its own. It is where the template stops and your code begins.
Meaning
The words that say what a folder of a template, or a member type, is for.
Member
A folder you add to a collection and name, such as Reservation in Context. It is part of the template, so adding one makes a revision.
Member type
What a template says once about every member of a collection: what a member is, and which folders it holds.
Missing
A folder the template expects that is not on disk. A kind of inconsistency the check reports.
Revision
One version of a template. Every change adds one, with a reason Barnark writes, and none is changed in place.
Shipped template
One of the six templates Barnark ships, the GUI’s Barnark scaffolds: read-only, and changed only by a new Barnark.
Slot
The command line’s word for a member type a folder offers. template:offers lists slots and template:new takes one.
Tag
A label a template gives its folders to say what part each plays, such as Domain. A folder carries the tags of its kind of folder.
Template
A description of what folders a project has and what each is for. Barnark says template for every one, the bound one included; the GUI calls it a scaffold.
Scaffold
What the GUI calls a template. Your scaffolds are the ones you copied, Barnark scaffolds the shipped ones, and an ark’s own scaffold is reached through its ark.
Unbound template
A template of yours that is not attached to any folder. It can be cloned into the project as an ark, or deleted.
Unexpected
A folder on disk that the template does not expect. A kind of inconsistency the check reports.
